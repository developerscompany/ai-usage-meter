4.0.1

Fixes for Kimi Code plans and for keys that show a message.

• Kimi Code plans without a weekly limit: Both Limits and All Services show the monthly limit in its place, marked MO.
• A key or login that was refused and then replaced is tried at once, not after up to ten minutes.
• Pressing a key that shows only a message asks again.
• A long reset time no longer runs into 100% on All Services and Both Limits.
• Claude on an account without a 5-hour limit: the key's settings say why.

4.0.0

New name: AI Usage Meter. The plugin now covers Claude, Codex and Kimi Code.

• Codex keys: 5-hour and weekly limits, credits, tokens, API cost, cache hits, model mix, status, and a key that opens Codex.
• Kimi Code keys: 5-hour, weekly and monthly limits, extra usage, status, and a key that opens Kimi Code.
• All Services: the three services side by side on one key.
• The logo of its service on every key; each key can hide it.
• Flipping pages of your Stream Deck no longer asks the services again.
• Fixes: Model Mix with a share under 1%, a tag on top of a long label, a login that was kept after signing in with another account.
